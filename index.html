About

Deep in the plumbing of payments

I build secure, scalable backend systems across fintech, banking, and payments — expert in Java and Spring Boot microservices, with a strong foundation in distributed systems and complex system debugging. My work spans core banking platforms, national payment switches, EMV chip migrations, and payment tokenization — including a 30% tokenization performance gain at Visa. I'm also experienced in Identity and Access Management (Keycloak, OAuth2/OIDC) and AML compliance systems.

It started in Khartoum, where I helped build APIs for Sudan's national payment switch before eventually landing at Visa.
