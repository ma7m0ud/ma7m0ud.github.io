About

What I work on

I'm a senior software engineer with 9+ years across Java and Spring Boot, distributed systems and system design — sharding, caching, load balancing, and the rest — plus security and compliance work including PCI DSS. Most of that experience sits in payments and banking: core banking platforms, national payment switches, EMV chip migrations, and payment tokenization, including a 30% tokenization performance gain at Visa. I'm also experienced in Identity and Access Management (Keycloak, OAuth2/OIDC) and AML compliance systems.
